## [v1.3.0] - 2026-10-17

### Added (Major Pro Coaching Feature Update)
- **Hands-Free "Countdown to Start" (`HandsFreeStartController`)**:
  - Automatically detects when the user steps 5–7 paces back into the camera view and holds a stable starting pose for ~1.2 seconds.
  - Launches an animated holographic `"3... 2... 1... GO!"` countdown ring with spoken voice countdown cues and starts recording the set completely hands-free—no need to walk back and forth to tap the screen.
  - Includes a smart 3.5-second post-set cooldown after finishing a set so users can rest or stand up without accidentally triggering a new countdown.
- **Beginner "Ghost Shadow" AR Form Overlay (`GhostShadowOverlay`)**:
  - Renders a translucent cyan-and-emerald holographic stickman silhouette directly over the live camera feed for all 35+ exercises (`drawStickmanDemonstration`).
  - Allows complete beginners to visually mirror ideal joint angles, depth, and posture in real time right on top of their live camera view.
  - Automatically synchronizes its movement cycle with the active Tempo Metronome preset when enabled.
- **Tempo & Eccentric Cadence Metronome (`rememberTempoMetronomeState`, `TempoMetronomeHudBar`)**:
  - Built-in audio-visual Time-Under-Tension (TUT) pacing engine with 5 selectable presets:
    - `OFF` (Free Pace)
    - `2-1-2` (Beginner Control — 2s Down, 1s Hold, 2s Up)
    - `3-1-1` (Hypertrophy TUT — 3s Eccentric, 1s Pause, 1s Explosive Drive)
    - `2-0-1` (Athletic Power — 2s Down, 0s Hold, 1s Drive)
    - `4-2-1` (Slow Burn — 4s Eccentric, 2s Isometric Hold, 1s Up)
  - Displays a live phase banner (`LOWER SLOWLY`, `HOLD DEPTH`, `DRIVE UP!`), phase progress bar, countdown timer, and spoken cadence cues (`"Down"`, `"Hold"`, `"Push"`).
- **Pro Coaching Quick-Toggle Strip & Controls Drawer Integration**:
  - Added a 3-chip quick-toggle strip (`Auto-Start`, `Ghost Form`, `Tempo`) directly beneath the live camera viewport plus full configuration cards inside the Camera & Coach Controls bottom sheet (`showControlsDrawer`) with persistent state in `CoachPreferences`.

## [v1.2.19] - 2026-10-17

### Added & Enhanced
- **Adaptive Range-of-Motion (ROM) Depth Calibration for Beginners**:
  - `PushUpAnalyzer` now adapts depth and lockout thresholds specifically per exercise variant:
    - **Wall Push-up (`push_up_wall`)**: Calibrated full-depth lockout at 106° (115° acceptance threshold) so complete beginners learning wall push-ups are immediately credited with valid reps rather than blocked by floor-pushup 92° angles.
    - **Incline & Knee Push-ups (`push_up_incline`, `knee_push_up`)**: Calibrated 98° full-depth target with 106° threshold, perfectly matching elevated surface mechanics.
    - **Standard Floor Push-ups**: Calibrated 92° target with beginner-friendly 98° completion threshold.
  - Dual-arm profile sensing using `min(leftElbowAngle, rightElbowAngle)` ensuring camera angle foreshortening never fails to register chest descent.
  - Contextual real-time voice and text guidance tailored for beginner exercises (e.g., "Lean closer to wall" vs. "Push lower • Incomplete depth").
- **Chair Squat & Incline Squat Detection Support (`SquatAnalyzer`)**:
  - Added exercise-aware depth calibration for chair and assisted squats (`112°` target) allowing beginners practicing chair squats to achieve verified reps without bottoming out past parallel.
  - Dual-knee `min(leftKneeAngle, rightKneeAngle)` tracking prevents false depth drops caused by camera profile occlusion.
- **Dynamic Rep Counting for Prone Movements (`bird_dog`)**:
  - Converted `bird_dog` from isometric hold mode to dynamic rep counting mode (`ExerciseFramingMode.PUSH_UP`) with live bilateral extension tracking.
- **Knee Landmark Fallback for Out-of-Frame Ankles (`PoseDetectorProcessor`, `PlankAnalyzer`)**:
  - Implemented automatic Shoulder-Hip-Knee fallback when user's feet/ankles are cropped by the camera frame, or during knee push-ups and knee planks. Prevents sudden tracking dropouts and false "hip sag" errors.
- **Eliminated False-Positive Standing Squat Detection (`ExerciseClassifier`)**:
  - Removed aggressive blanket fallback that classified standing idle in front of the camera as squats.
  - Added standing Wall Push-up classification (`push_up_wall`) based on arm height and elbow flexion.
  - Guarded active workout routines against unexpected auto-switching while users are training scheduled sets.

### UI Revamp & Polish
- **Missed-Session Emergency Banner Revamp**:
  - Redesigned missed training day banner into a high-contrast amber alert surface with rounded iconography, clear typography hierarchy, and proportional action buttons (`Catch Up Today`, `To Rest Day`, `Skip`).
- **Live Motion Coach HUD Alignment & Feedback Cleanliness**:
  - Seamlessly synced active exercise IDs between camera processor, pose analyzers, and on-screen HUD telemetry overlays.

## [v1.2.16] - 2026-10-13

### Fixed
- **Weekly Schedule Repeating Day Duplication**:
  - Resolved root cause where non-deterministic UUID primary keys in `ScheduledWorkoutEntity` caused multiple sets of weekly schedules to accumulate in SQLite across app launches and schedule updates.
  - Replaced random UUID generation with deterministic date-bound primary keys (`schedule_${dateString}`) ensuring strict idempotency and `OnConflictStrategy.REPLACE` replacement.
  - Added Room DAO deduplication query `pruneDuplicateDates()` that automatically cleans existing legacy databases of duplicate calendar entries on startup.
  - Enforced `GROUP BY dateString ORDER BY dateString ASC` on all Room queries for weekly schedules and flows.
- **Weekly Schedule Strip Layout & Overflow**:
  - Replaced fixed width day pills (`42.dp`) with adaptive symmetrical weight distribution (`Modifier.weight(1f).height(64.dp)` with `Arrangement.spacedBy(4.dp)`).
  - Guarantees exactly 7 distinct days (Monday through Sunday) fit cleanly across any screen size without horizontal overflow or hidden days.
- **Missed Session Banner Recovery**:
  - Resolved issue where missed workout warnings remained stuck after actioning because stale duplicate entries remained in `SCHEDULED` status.
  - Deduplicated missed session detection and resolution routines so resolving updates the exact calendar date cleanly.
- **Workout-Day Consistency Streak (Schedule-Aware Streaks)**:
  - Fixed streak calculation to count consecutive **workout days** according to the athlete's scheduled active days (`availableDaysCsv`) rather than strict calendar days.
  - Scheduled rest days between workouts (e.g. Tuesdays/Thursdays/weekends for Mon-Wed-Fri routines) no longer break the training streak.
  - Streaks only reset when an athlete actually misses a scheduled workout day.
  - Updated streak UI in History screen to display `"X Workouts"` with contextual motivational status.
