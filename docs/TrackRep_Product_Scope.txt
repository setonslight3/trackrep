TrackRep Product Scope

TrackRep • Developed by Setons

Product Vision

TrackRep is a local-first Android workout app focused on full-body muscle development. Its defining feature is Track, an adaptive AI assistant combined with live camera-based exercise tracking.

Initial Scope

Android only initially, targeting the Infinix Smart 9. One APK for the first test release.

Future releases can add broader Android ABI support, iOS and possibly desktop.

No account or cloud database initially. Workout data stays on the device.

Users can export/import a versioned backup file to move their data.

Gemini powers Track's reasoning and conversation; exercise vision/counting is primarily on-device.

Dark theme: black + gold. Light theme: white + red.

Onboarding

Goal, with full-body muscle development as the primary goal.

Five experience levels: Beginner, Novice, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert.

Equipment: no equipment, some equipment, or full gym; custom equipment can be entered.

Available days, preferred morning/evening time and workout duration.

Optional live camera tracking and required permissions only when needed.

All preferences remain editable later.

Workout System

Balanced training across chest, shoulders, arms, back, core, glutes, quads, hamstrings and calves.

Bodyweight exercises are always available; equipment-aware alternatives are selected when equipment exists.

Plans account for level, equipment, recovery, preferences and previous performance.

Rest days and muscle recovery are protected by scheduling rules.

Adaptive AI

After each session: Too Easy, Just Right, Difficult, or Could Not Complete.

Optional reasons for difficulty or skipping.

Camera metrics can influence progression: reps, partial reps, range of motion, rep speed, form consistency and fatigue indicators.

Missed sessions can be rescheduled without creating excessive workout stacking.

Track explains meaningful changes to the plan.

Track AI

Conversational assistant for questions, exercise explanations, plan changes and scheduling.

Controlled actions: create/modify workout, replace exercise, change reps/sets/rest, reschedule, add rest day and set reminders.

AI actions use approved application functions, not unrestricted database access.

Track reports what it changed and why.

Track does not diagnose injuries or provide medical treatment.

Live Camera Coach

TrackRep's signature feature.

Exercise-specific camera instructions and a short setup tutorial.

Live preview validates approximate framing, body visibility, angle and tracking confidence.

Initial MVP exercises: push-up first, then squat and plank.

Live rep counting, partial/invalid rep detection, basic form feedback, range-of-motion analysis, rep timing and performance/fatigue indicators.

Feedback should be specific, such as 'Keep your hips aligned'.

Camera processing should remain on-device where technically feasible.

Timers, Progress and Backup

Rep-based exercises show live progress; timed exercises have an exercise timer; rest periods have a separate timer.

Store workout history, progression and camera-derived metrics locally.

Export/import versioned backups. Optional readable workout sharing.

No cloud account required initially.

Privacy and Future Scope

Camera permission only for camera tracking. Notifications only when enabled.

Explain sensitive permissions clearly.

Do not upload raw camera footage by default.

Future: more exercises, wider Android support, iOS, optional encrypted cloud sync, wearables and desktop companion.

MVP Success Criterion

TrackRep must demonstrate reliable, understandable live tracking for the first supported exercise on the target phone before the full product is completed.
